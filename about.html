贅沢

Zeitaku is the Japanese word for luxury. Not the loud kind. The kind you notice when a jacket sits right on your shoulders, when a trouser falls straight, when a suit you bought years ago still looks like the best thing in the room.

ZEITAKU makes that kind of suit for women. We draft every pattern on a woman's frame, choose fabrics we can name, and keep the colours to four: obsidian, champagne, taupe and ivory. Burgundy is the lining and nothing more.

We make fewer pieces and make them well. That's the whole idea.
